How it works

You talk. We do the rest.

  1. 1

    You talk

    Twenty to thirty minutes a week. A call, or voice notes from the airport.

  2. 2

    We write

    We pull out the opinions worth publishing, add the market context and draft it the way you would say it.

  3. 3

    You approve

    Change a line or send a yes. Nothing is published without it.

  4. 4

    We publish

    From your account, on schedule. Then we report the conversations it started.

Questions

Asked on every first call.

Anything else, bring it to the call.

Will it sound like me?

Like you on a good day. We write from your calls and voice notes, and you change anything that feels off. If a draft does not sound like you, it does not go out.

Do you use AI to write it?

No. People write your posts, from what you actually said.

How much of my time does it take?

About thirty minutes a week: one short call or a few voice notes, then a few minutes to approve drafts.

Will anyone know you write for me?

Not unless you tell them. We never name a client without permission, and we will sign an NDA before the first working session if you want one.

How long before it works?

Expect engagement to move in six to eight weeks and real conversations in three to six months.

I run a listed company. What about disclosure rules?

Nothing is published without your approval, so your compliance or investor relations team can review every post before it goes out.

It is where buyers, investors and candidates go to check you.
